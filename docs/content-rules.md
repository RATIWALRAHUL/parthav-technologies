# Parthav Technologies — Content, Copywriting & Credibility Rules

This document outlines the editorial voice, tone, and strict authenticity guidelines for all copy, assets, and messaging on Parthav Technologies digital properties.

---

## 1. Brand Positioning & Core Message

- **Brand**: PARTHAV TECHNOLOGIES
- **Tagline**: Ideas to Impact.
- **Positioning**: Digital experiences. Built for growth.
- **Geographic Identity**: Built in Jaipur. Designed for the world.
- **Tone of Voice**:
  - Confident, deliberate, and clear.
  - Free from hyperbole and marketing buzzwords.
  - Demonstrates engineering excellence and design precision.
  - Editorial, concise, and focused on business value.

---

## 2. Strict Authenticity & Anti-Fabrication Rules

> ### 🛑 ZERO FABRICATION POLICY
> Parthav Technologies maintains strict integrity in its public representations. Under no circumstances should AI agents or contributors invent fake business credentials.

1. **Client Logos**:
   - Never claim unverified Fortune 500 companies as clients.
   - Where client logos are shown as visual placeholders in trust bars, use explicit category statements:
     `DESIGN-LED • ENGINEERING-DRIVEN • GROWTH-FOCUSED`.
2. **Case Studies & Results**:
   - Only publish real metrics where client permission is documented.
   - For illustrative or demo case studies, clearly mark them as:
     `CASE STUDY PREVIEW` or `Representative project data`.
3. **Testimonials**:
   - Never invent fictional quotes or fake client personas.
   - Use clearly designated placeholder badges (`Client Testimonial Placeholder`) until authorized client quotes are provided.
4. **Company Metrics**:
   - Use conservative, verified values only (e.g. `50+ Projects Delivered`, `4.9/5 Client Rating`, `95+ Lighthouse Target`).
   - Do not claim decades of experience, hundreds of employees, or fabricated funding rounds.
5. **Contact Information**:
   - Primary official email: `hello@parthavtechnologies.com`
   - Location: `Jaipur, India`
   - Working hours: `Mon – Sat, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM`
   - Never hardcode private mobile numbers or fabricated street addresses.

## 3. SEO Content Guidelines

- Target primary terms naturally: `UI/UX design agency Jaipur`, `web development company Jaipur`, `SEO agency Jaipur`, `AI solutions company Jaipur`.
- Never keyword-stuff.
- Maintain natural, human readability with structured headings (`H1`, `H2`, `H3`).
- Provide descriptive, helpful copy that answers real client questions.
